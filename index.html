FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my exact age?

Enter your date of birth (day, month, year) into AgeVerse and click Calculate. You will instantly see your exact age in years, months, days, hours and minutes.

How many days old am I?

AgeVerse calculates your total days alive from your date of birth to today. Simply enter your birthday and click Calculate to find out instantly.

What is my zodiac sign by date of birth?

Your zodiac sign is determined by your date of birth. AgeVerse automatically shows your zodiac sign, element, ruling planet, lucky number and birthstone after you enter your birthday.

How many days until my next birthday?

AgeVerse shows a live birthday countdown — the exact number of days until your next birthday — every time you calculate your age.

Is this age calculator free?

Yes! AgeVerse is completely free to use. No sign-up required. Just enter your date of birth and instantly get your age, zodiac, life stats and birthday countdown.
